JobPilot
A personal job-search assistant: one private place to track every application, and an AI that helps you prepare for the next step.

Think of it like a social feed, but with no friends and no strangers. Just you and your AI helper.

Status: 🚧 In planning / early development

The Problem
Job hunting quickly turns into a messy Excel sheet: dozens of rows, endless columns, and no easy way to see the bigger picture. It is hard to remember:

Which companies you applied to, and when
Which cover letter and CV version you sent to each one
Who replied, who ghosted you, and who rejected you
What you need to prepare when an interview invitation lands in your inbox
The Idea
JobPilot replaces the spreadsheet with a personal website that tracks your applications and analyzes them for you. An AI assistant looks at the job requirements, your CV, and your cover letter, then tells you what to focus on next.

Planned Features
1. Application Tracker
Log each application: company, role, date, link, status, notes
Attach the CV version and cover letter used for each job
Statuses such as Applied, Replied, Interview, Offer, Rejected, No Response
2. Analytics Dashboard
Total applications sent (per week / per month)
Response rate and interview rate
Replies by company
Which cover letter / CV version performs best
Status breakdown charts
3. Email Reply Tracking
Record replies from companies and link them to the matching application
Flag emails that need action (e.g. interview invitations)
4. AI Interview Prep
When an interview email is logged, the AI compares:
the job requirements
your CV
your cover letter
It then suggests what to revise, which skills to brush up on, and likely questions to practice
5. Feedback Log
Save feedback from recruiters or interviewers after each stage
Use it to improve future applications
6. Private by Design
Single-user, no social features
Your data stays yours
Example Workflow
You apply to a Data Analyst role and log it in JobPilot with the CV and cover letter you used.
A week later the company replies. You log the email and the status updates to Interview.
JobPilot reads the job description, your CV, and your cover letter, then gives you a prep checklist.
After the interview, you enter the feedback. The dashboard updates and the insights improve.
